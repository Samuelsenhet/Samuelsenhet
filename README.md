## Hey, I'm Samuel

I build software products end to end, mostly alone, from Sweden. One of them is in the App Store.

I take a product the whole way rather than stopping at a prototype: the interface, the app, the database and its access rules, the migrations, the release builds, the store listing in two languages, and the review submissions. It is slower than specialising, but nothing about what I ship is a mystery to me.

### What I'm building

**[MÄÄK](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/id6789912827)** is live on the App Store, on version 1.0.7. A Swedish dating app that matches on personality instead of photographs: it proposes a few people a day, some who resemble you and some who complement you, and there is no infinite grid to scroll. Built with Expo on top of Supabase, with phone sign-in and realtime chat.

**Bibelrösten** is in development. A Swedish voice you call and talk with for five minutes, with no account and no chat log. The hard part is the promise that when it says "the Bible says", the words really are in the Bible, so the model is not allowed to write them. The model writes a reference and a control layer between speech recognition and speech synthesis inserts the verified text.

**Crava** is in development. A second-hand marketplace running backwards: buyers post the exact thing they want and sellers send offers, with BankID verification and escrowed payment so two strangers can finish the trade.

### How I work

Mostly TypeScript, React and React Native, Expo, and Postgres by way of Supabase. I care much more about what a product does to the person using it than about which framework it was built with.

Most of what I build is closed source today. That is a decision about timing rather than a position on open source, and I would rather say so plainly than label a private repository as something it is not.
